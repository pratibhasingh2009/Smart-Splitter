Introduction

GLBITM • CSE
01

Domain & Background

  • FinTech & Distributed Web Systems engineering domain.
  • Shared living expenses are routine for college roommates, project groups, and traveling peers.
  • Manual calculation creates confusion, misremembered debts, and social friction.
02

Problem Statement

  • Messy paper logs and group chat messages lead to missed records and forgotten settlements.
  • Circular debts result in unnecessary multiple peer transactions (A owes B, B owes C, C owes A).
  • Existing market apps lock vital settlement features behind paid monthly subscriptions.
03

Motivation & Significance

  • Build a fast, ad-free, transparent platform specifically tailored for students and shared groups.
  • Algorithmic debt optimization reducing multi-party payments to minimal direct transfers.
  • Native UPI payment links and instant real-time synchronization via Google Firestore.

Greedy Settlement Algorithm

GLBITM • CSE

Greedy Min-Cash-Flow Formulation

1. Net Balance Calculation:
For each member i: Net[i] = Sum(Paid) - Sum(Owed). The zero-sum property guarantees: Σ Net[i] == 0.00.
2. Partition & Sort:
Divide into Debtors (Net < 0) and Creditors (Net > 0). Sort descending by absolute values.
3. Greedy Pairing:
Pair the largest debtor with the largest creditor. Settle min(|Debt|, |Credit|). Reduces transactions from O(N²) to at most N-1!

Before vs After Optimization

Pratibha paid ₹1,200 (Food) • Pratiksha paid ₹600 (Cab) • Priyansh paid ₹400 (Groceries).
